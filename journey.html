Career

From Product Analyst to Associate Product Manager.

  1. Where it started

    Feb 2022 – Feb 2023

    Product Analyst, TartanHQ

    Owned
    Payslip OCR model training on Nanonets, payroll data pipelines, and early HRMS integrations, supporting real-time income verification for fintech lenders.
    Impact
    Reduced manual payslip processing effort by 80%. Built the foundation for HRMS Sync that would go on to cover 20+ payroll systems.
  2. Promoted

    Feb 2023 – Dec 2023

    Associate Product Manager, TartanHQ

    Owned
    HRMS Sync end-to-end, a unified payroll API product covering 20+ systems. Led onboarding of anchor clients: Jupiter Money, Qube Health, and Jelly Bean.
    Impact
    Reduced manual payroll operations by 80%. Improved end-user conversion by 27%. Partnered on GTM, contributing to a 20% lift in qualified leads.
  3. Sole PM

    Mar 2024 – Jul 2024

    Associate Product Manager, Pumper

    Owned
    Everything, as the only PM. Led the Built for Shopify certification, redesigned the onboarding funnel using Mixpanel, and drove the free-to-paid model transition.
    Impact
    Earned Built for Shopify badge, lifting sign-ups +20%. Reduced onboarding drop-off by 30%. Converted 1,000+ merchants to paid in one month.
  4. New chapter

    Aug 2024 – Nov 2024

    Product & Content Specialist, Prezent.ai

    Owned
    Built BP Genius and curated a 35K+ Best Practice Library. Contributed to ASTRID's BioPharma Contextual Intelligence and ran AI model evaluations (BLIP, Gemini, GPT-4V).
    Impact
    Production time cut 75% (4h → 30min). BioPharma feature hit 500K+ generations and 100K+ downloads. Gemini adoption cut AI costs ~97%.
  5. Promoted

    Dec 2024 – Present

    Associate Product Manager, Prezent.ai

    Owned
    Lightning Studio (brand template automation), AI Search & Discovery across 20M+ slides, Astrid Suggestions and Try Searching For (LLM-powered search guidance), and Max plan subscription expansion.
    Impact
    "No Results" searches cut from 10% → <1%. 20,000+ users in month one (zero paid acquisition). Template conversion: 3–4 days → seconds.
