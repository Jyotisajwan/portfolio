Lightning Studio

Turning a 3–4 day manual brand-template conversion process into a workflow that completes in seconds.

3–4 days → secondsTemplate conversion time
~97%AI captioning cost reduction
~58%Re-indexing time saved

The Problem

When a client introduced a new brand template or updated an existing one, designers had to manually convert slides into brand-compliant shells. The work took around 3–4 days and created a dependency on design capacity.

The Stakes

Every new or updated template added another manual conversion task. This slowed client enablement and made the process difficult to scale as more brands and templates were introduced.

The Job

Make template conversion repeatable and dramatically faster, so clients could move from a new brand template to usable, brand-compliant shells without waiting days for manual design work.

How

The workflow converts slide assets into structured SVG-based assets and runs them through an automated conversion pipeline that applies the required brand structure and layouts. The resulting shells can be generated in seconds.

For the related image and slide captioning workflow, I evaluated BLIP, Gemini and GPT-4V and selected Gemini based on the evaluation. This contributed to approximately 97% lower AI captioning cost and 58% less re-indexing time.

Impact

Template conversion moved from a 3–4 day manual process to seconds, reducing the turnaround time and dependency on manual designer effort. The captioning workflow also reduced AI cost and re-indexing time.

Prezent.ai·Platform PM·Project case study

Search & Discovery

Built a novel slide search experience and reduced the “no results” rate from 10% to under 1%.

<1%No-results rate, down from 10%
99%Of 1,000 real queries solved in evaluation
$1.2MAt-risk account retained

The Problem

Users struggled to find slides they knew already existed. Traditional keyword search could miss content when the query did not match the wording or concepts represented on a slide.

The Stakes

A $1.2M account was frustrated because its teams could not find existing slides. On a content platform, an asset users cannot discover loses much of its value. The experience needed to work across 150+ enterprise accounts.

The Job

Redefine slide search so users could discover relevant content through both the language they used and the meaning and visual context of the slide, without slowing the platform down.

How

At upload, vision models analyse each slide and Gemini Flash extracts metadata covering imagery, text, visual flow and business concepts. This enriched metadata is stored in a vector database.

At search time, Apache Solr combines exact keyword matching with semantic retrieval using embeddings. Results are then ranked for relevance, including whether the user is looking for visuals or data. The workflow was evaluated against 1,000 real queries.

Impact

The no-results rate dropped from 10% to under 1%. In the 1,000-query evaluation, 99% of queries were solved. The improvement helped address the discoverability issue affecting a $1.2M account.

Astrid Suggestions & LLM-Powered Search

Helping users discover the right search terms when they are unsure what to look for.

20,000+Unique users in month one
500K+Auto Generator runs
100K+BioPharma downloads

The Problem

Users often knew the kind of slide they needed but did not know the exact words to search for. A search experience that depended too heavily on the user’s wording could make relevant content difficult to discover.

The Stakes

When users cannot translate their intent into an effective query, they may miss useful slides or abandon the search. This creates friction in content discovery and reduces the value of the existing library.

The Job

Guide users towards better queries by using the context available in slide metadata and language models, while supporting multiple levels of matching.

How

The search journey supports exact matching, partial matching and semantic matching. When a query does not return a useful match, Try Searching For can suggest related terms, synonyms or alternative queries based on the user’s intent and available context.

Astrid Suggestions adds contextual query guidance so users have a starting point even when they are unsure how to phrase their search. Separately, BioPharma-focused Contextual Intelligence enriched the Auto Generator’s knowledge base with industry context.

Impact

The product reached 20,000+ unique users in its first month without paid acquisition. The BioPharma-related usage figures shared for this work include 500K+ Auto Generator runs and 100K+ downloads.

Prezent.ai·Product Specialist / APM L2

Support Knowledge Agent

An AI knowledge-base chatbot that answers from company documentation and escalates genuine bugs to Jira.

Self-serviceFaster answers from internal knowledge
JiraAutomated bug escalation

The Problem

Support requests and the ticket backlog were growing. Users needed a reliable way to find answers, while genuine bug reports took too long to reach the teams responsible for fixing them.

The Stakes

Without a knowledge-based self-service option, support teams had to spend time answering repeat questions. Bug reports also risked getting delayed in the support queue before reaching the right owners.

The Job

Create an AI knowledge-base agent that could answer questions using company documentation and automatically route genuine bugs to Jira with the relevant context.

How

Release notes, FAQs, Confluence pages and past support history are used as the knowledge base. The chatbot retrieves answers from this company knowledge rather than guessing. Known questions receive an immediate response, while genuine bugs are escalated to Jira automatically with the relevant details attached.

Impact

The workflow is designed to reduce repetitive support requests, ease ticket backlog pressure and speed up bug handoff through automated Jira escalation.
